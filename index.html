Campus traffic logger

No public source says how congestion around campus changes through the day, so I built one. Every 30 minutes a script records live speeds on 12 roads into campus, each placed on an Arkansas DOT count station so speeds line up with traffic volumes. It started on my laptop, then moved to a scheduled GitHub Actions job so it runs whether my laptop is open or not.

My part: designed and built the logger, picked and checked the 12 road points, and pulled 40 years of ArDOT counts. Its data drives the map above and HillPark's traffic forecast.
